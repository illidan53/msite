Privacy

Stock Workbench (finance.nphunter.net) shows public US market data. You can use it without an account. Signing in is optional and only lets you keep your own version of the watchlists.

What we receive when you sign in

You can sign in with Google or Microsoft. We ask only for the standard openid, email and profile permissions. From the sign-in response we keep your provider's account identifier (Google's subject id, or Microsoft's tenant and object ids), your email address and your name. We do not get access to your mailbox, contacts, files or any other data, and we never see your password.

How we use it

  • The account identifier links you to your saved watchlist edits.
  • Your email and name are shown to you so you know which account you used.
  • A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary for signing in, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

We do not sell, share or rent this information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not send you email because you signed in.

Where it is stored

Your edits and session records are stored on our server in the AWS us-east-1 region. Only the site operator can access them. Session cookies are stored as one-way hashes.

Deleting your data

While signed in, open the account menu and choose Delete my data. This permanently removes your saved edits and signs you out on every device. You can also remove the app's access from your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps) or your Microsoft account (Privacy → Apps and services).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page.

隐私说明

股票观察台（finance.nphunter.net）展示公开的美股行情数据，无需注册即可使用。登录是可选的，只用于保存属于你自己的板块列表。

登录时我们获取什么

你可以使用 Google 或 Microsoft 账户登录。我们只申请标准的 openid、email、profile 权限，并保存登录结果中的账户标识（Google 的 subject，或 Microsoft 的租户 ID 与对象 ID）、邮箱和姓名。我们无法访问你的邮件、联系人、文件或其他任何数据，也看不到你的密码。

如何使用

  • 账户标识用于关联你保存的板块编辑。
  • 邮箱和姓名只展示给你本人，方便确认登录的是哪个账户。
  • 会话 Cookie 让你最多保持登录 30 天；它是登录所必需的，我们不使用任何广告或追踪 Cookie。

我们不会出售、分享或出租这些信息，不用于广告，也不会因为你登录而给你发邮件。

存储位置

你的编辑和会话记录保存在我们位于 AWS us-east-1 区域的服务器上，只有站点运营者可以访问；会话 Cookie 只以单向哈希保存。

删除数据

登录后打开账户菜单，选择删除我的数据，即可永久删除你保存的编辑，并在所有设备上退出登录。你也可以在 Google 账户（安全性 → 第三方应用）或 Microsoft 账户（隐私 → 应用和服务）中撤销本站的访问权限。

变更

本说明如有变更，会在此页面发布更新后的版本。
