# Watchlist universe review

Reviewed 2026-09-20. Supersedes the earlier four-list-only review. Twelve sector/theme lists have now been reviewed; the explicit user-defined close-watch list is preserved. The four primary menu entries and nine lists inside Other remain.

## Evidence and its limits

The user requested a universe reflecting X discussions and company businesses. Live X search redirected to login; available public search results were incomplete and often old. We therefore do not claim to have measured the most popular stocks today. This is a manually curated candidate universe combining dated discussion samples, business coverage and explicit user interests. Retained comparison anchors are not claimed to be social leaders.

Searches targeted each existing theme and candidate cashtags. Exclude spam with long ticker dumps, unrelated names (for example Zeta AI versus Zeta Global), automated Grok analyses, and third-party mirrors as ranking evidence. Earnings-calendar posts are weak discovery signals, not independent enthusiasm. A promotional portfolio post is disclosed as such; a repost does not count as another independent opinion. No likes, views or repeated appearances were converted to a numerical score. We could not establish comparable 7-day/30-day author counts or sentiment.

Each source link is dated where a publication date is available; dates follow the indexed page’s rendered date, not a claimed uniform UTC window. Company sources confirm business exposure; they do not establish investor popularity. The very old 2024 growth-portfolio sample and 2025 healthcare/financial samples are historical discovery only. Sparse evidence in staples and energy is explicitly described in the UI.

## Business classification

All selected symbols were checked against the existing provider’s active ticker reference and company description. These reference summaries are secondary company profiles, not official statements. Selected official company pages and disclosures below were also reviewed, particularly for the newly added AI cloud, satellite, healthcare, stablecoin and nuclear businesses. Exact group assignments are editorial judgments, not claimed GICS classifications.

- CRWV and NBIS: direct AI-cloud businesses. IREN: AI cloud with mining transition exposure. APLD/WULF/CIFR/CORZ/HUT: separate data-center development, hosting or mixed compute group; their models and delivery stages differ.
- Separate chip supply, cloud operation, servers, networking, cooling/electrical equipment, REITs and storage. Remove AMT/CCI tower exposure from AI cloud and keep power generators in the power list.
- NOK remains explicitly adjacent optical-network equipment; keep user priorities LITE/NOK/CRDO/ALAB. SNDK adds NAND/SSD exposure; WDC is hard-drive exposure.
- APP/ZETA/TTD: advertising software. DUOL: learning software. HIMS/TEM/RXRX: care, diagnostics and drug discovery, not generic SaaS. ASTS: satellite communications; RKLB/LUNR/RDW/PL: space industry.
- Broaden names to Industrials, Space & Defense and Power & Energy Transition so developers and equipment companies are not mislabeled as traditional contractors or regulated utilities.
- One share class per issuer within each list: retain GOOGL and drop GOOG. Replace legacy PSTG with P after issuer confirmation. Remove HES/MRO/PARA/IPG from the former lists; no blind use of stale ticker histories.

## Data check

All 226 unique selected symbols, including close-watch, had active ticker references and 20 available daily bars ending 2026-09-18. Each exceeded $20 million in estimated mean daily traded value; observed minimum $47,850,457. Estimate = mean(daily close × daily share volume), not exact trade-by-trade turnover. This establishes data availability/liquidity, not popularity or investment merit. No additional paid source was introduced.

The machine-readable review in [watchlist-universe-review.json](watchlist-universe-review.json) lists every symbol, company website returned by the provider, classification, dated discussion links where explicitly matched, and the data check. Company URLs in that ledger are provider references; not every URL was independently opened. Empty discussion evidence means no measured X support, not zero public interest.

## Maintenance

This is a dated manual review, not a live social scanner. Repeat issuer/ticker, business and liquidity checks on membership changes. A genuinely current popularity ranking would require a comparable recent X sample with unique-author counts, deduplication, time windows and spam filtering. That data is currently unavailable; no background update or paid subscription has been configured.
